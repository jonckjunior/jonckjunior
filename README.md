### Adilson Jonck

Backend/infra engineer at Google. On the side I build multiplayer netcode and small games in LÖVE2D and PICO-8.

**Featured**

- **[rollback-arena](https://github.com/jonckjunior/rollback-arena)**: rollback netcode from scratch, with input prediction, snapshot rollback, per-frame desync detection and cross-platform determinism. Fully tested.
- **[arena-fighter](https://github.com/jonckjunior/arena-fighter)**: the playable game, a two-player online shooter-platformer with deterministic lockstep netcode.
- **[Yet Another Pico Tetris](https://github.com/jonckjunior/Yet-Another-Pico-Tetris)**: SRS-compliant Tetris for PICO-8 with T-spins and 10 challenge modes. [Play it](https://www.lexaloffle.com/bbs/?tid=155299)
- **[Procedural animation with Verlet physics](https://github.com/jonckjunior/procedural-animation-tutorial)**: a PICO-8 devlog and tutorial. [Try it](https://www.lexaloffle.com/bbs/?tid=154658)
- **[Blink to Reload](https://github.com/jonckjunior/blink_to_reload)**: a PICO-8 boss rush where dashing through a blink is your reload. [Play it](https://www.lexaloffle.com/bbs/?tid=153709)
